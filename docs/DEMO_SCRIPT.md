# SupportNova Demo Script

1. Sign in as Administrator and show Dashboard, Knowledge Base, Rule Matrix, Reports and Review Queue.
2. Upload a PDF policy and show extracted content plus traceable chunks/page references.
3. Sign in as Customer and submit a normal delivery complaint.
4. Submit a safety complaint and show P0/Critical escalation.
5. Submit a prompt-injection complaint and show manual-review routing.
6. Submit a repeated/near-duplicate complaint and show repeat detection.
7. Open a manual-review case, approve or escalate it, and show the audit trail.
8. Open Reports and explain that SLA compliance is calculated from persisted timestamps rather than a hard-coded KPI.
9. Export CSV and demonstrate role-based data isolation.
